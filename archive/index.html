the problem

the Indian group travel
industry has a problem
nobody talks about.

01
same itinerary, every year
the same Spiti route has been running since 2019. new is harder to sell, so nobody takes the risk. you end up paying for an experience designed for someone else, five years ago.
02
can you pay? you're in.
most group trips have one filter: your bank balance. so you show up and spend four days with 24 strangers who have nothing in common except a bank transfer. the vibe is off. always.
03
bigger batches, worse trips
bigger batches mean better margins. so trips become crowds, crowds become chaos, and the destination gets lost somewhere between matching t-shirts and a WhatsApp group with 40 people in it.

how it works

deliberate by design.
not by accident.

step 01
you apply
a short application. no essays, no follower count. we want to know who you are as a traveler, not how impressive your resume is. takes 2 minutes.
step 02
we say yes
every application is read by a human. we look for curiosity, adaptability, and the kind of person who makes a trip better for everyone around them.
step 03
we build your batch
we match you with the right people, not just any available slot. small groups, intentional curation. the right ten people in the right place beats forty strangers on a bus.
step 04
you go somewhere real
an itinerary that doesn't exist in the market. places three pages deep on Google — or not listed at all. experience first. destination second.
